Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our solar mounting products

What types of solar mounting systems do you offer?

We offer comprehensive solar mounting solutions including roof mounts (color steel, tile, flat roof), ground mounts, solar carports, tracking systems, and all essential components like rails, clamps, and ground screws.

How long is your product warranty?

All our solar mounting products come with a 10-year warranty and a 25-year service life. We also offer extended warranty options for specific applications.

Do you provide OEM/ODM services?

Yes, we offer full OEM and ODM services. Our engineering team can design custom mounting solutions based on your specific requirements and brand needs.

What is your production capacity?

Our annual production capacity exceeds 12GW, making us one of the largest solar mounting manufacturers in Asia with two production bases in Xiamen and Zhangzhou.
